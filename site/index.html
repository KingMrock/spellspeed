Play DuelingBook at Spell Speed 2.

Spellspeed is a free extension that makes DuelingBook faster and easier on the eyes. Drag cards to play them, see your whole Deck at a glance, rewind any replay, and build decks with a search that finds the right card.

Main Deck 6 cards

Six Spells for DuelingBook. Every action they take is one of DuelingBook's own; they just resolve faster.

Drag and Drop

[Continuous Spell]

Drag a card to a zone to Summon, Set, attack, send it to the GY, banish it or return it to the Deck. Hold Shift for Defense Position, Alt to Set, or Ctrl to Normal Summon. You can also right-click a card to open its full action menu, or keep DuelingBook's hover menu.

You can negate this card's effects in the settings.

Card Fans

[Continuous Spell]

Your Deck, Extra Deck, GY and banished cards open as fans or grids you can search, sort, move and resize. When card text names a card, an archetype or a stat ("Level 4 or lower"), you can click it to list every card that matches.

You can negate this card's effects in the settings.

Deck Builder

[Normal Spell]

Search by type, frame, Attribute, ATK/DEF and effect, such as hand traps, searchers and negation. Access: list the cards that can add this card from your Deck. Summon Lab: list what your monsters can Summon, and what each recipe still needs. Undo with Ctrl Z.

Replay Timeline

[Quick-Play Spell]

During any replay: slide to any play, step back one play or one turn, or play at 1× to 4×. Moving back to a play you have already seen is instant.

Rulings

The questions players ask first.

Does Spellspeed play for me?

No. It automates no plays and adds no card effects. Every action is one you choose, sent through DuelingBook's own duel commands, as if you had clicked DuelingBook's buttons.

Spellspeed is an unofficial extension, not affiliated with or endorsed by DuelingBook.

Is it free?

Yes, and open source. There is nothing to buy and no account to create.

Can it see my password or my decks?

It never reads your password or login cookies, and it sends nothing to us. It works with the duel and the deck on the page, in your browser. Details are in the privacy policy.

I use DuelingBook Overflow or Dueling Book Enhancer.

Turn off DuelingBook Overflow: both change the same parts of the page. Dueling Book Enhancer works alongside Spellspeed; turn off its Advanced Replay Controls, since Spellspeed brings its own replay timeline.

Firefox, Safari or iPhone?

Firefox is next, on computers and on Android. Safari and iPhone are not supported yet. Today Spellspeed runs in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera on a computer, and as an Android app.

I don't like a change. Can I turn it off?

Every feature has its own switch in the settings (the Spellspeed button in your browser's toolbar), and each category can be turned off at once. Turning a feature off brings back DuelingBook's own behaviour.

Something broke. Where do I report it?

In the bug reports on our Discord, or by email to contact@spellspeed.com. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened; for replays, the replay link and the play number help most.
