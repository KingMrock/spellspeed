Overflow credits eyal282's chrome-ext-dueling-book-unlock as its original
framework, also under GPL v3:
https://github.com/eyal282/chrome-ext-dueling-book-unlock

Spellspeed's animation configuration and Deck/Extra Deck/replay integration are
adapted from the behavior and API calls in those sources. The drag gesture,
native-menu action selection, settings UI, and small loader are new code.
No Overflow dark-theme styles, card pools, assets, or automated card effects
are included. Spellspeed adds its own contextual menu using native available actions.

The fan interaction was inspired by the Fuwalab simulator, https://fuwalab.fr/.
Its implementation is original; no Fuwalab code, styles or assets are included.
Card artwork in the fan comes from DuelingBook's already-rendered viewer.

The dark themes in night.css (Night, Abyss, Ember) and the Classic dark text
boxes are Spellspeed's own rules and colours.

DuelingBook's public client v932 was inspected to verify native integration
points. That site's JavaScript and artwork are not distributed in this package.
This project is not affiliated with DuelingBook or Konami or Overflow, and is not endorsed by them.

The 39 sfx-*.wav effects are original synthesized sounds, generated by
Spellspeed's own sound scripts and licensed GPL-3.0-only with this project.
No third-party sound recordings are bundled. DuelingBook's original loss
clips and profile/intro music remain native and are not redistributed.

Card lookups: the YGOR ↗ button on the card preview opens a search on
YGOResources, https://db.ygoresources.com/, the card database of the
YGOrganization team (https://ygorganization.com/), using its documented
search link. No YGOResources data or code is bundled; nothing is requested
until the button is clicked. Thanks to its maintainers.
