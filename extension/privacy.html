Spellspeed privacy policy

Effective 16 October 2026 · Updated 21 October 2026 · Extension version 0.6.6

Spellspeed is an independent extension that adds interface controls and shortcuts to DuelingBook. It is not affiliated with DuelingBook or Konami.

Information used on your device

The extension reads the active DuelingBook page, including card and duel state, player names and relevant duel-chat messages, to display its controls and perform the actions you request. This information is processed in the page and is not collected by the extension developer. Spellspeed does not read your cookies.

Your DuelingBook login

DuelingBook keeps your username and password in the page so it can reconnect after a dropped connection. Spellspeed uses them in one place only: the Close it and connect button shown when DuelingBook says you are “already logged in” elsewhere. Only when you press it, Spellspeed sends DuelingBook's own reconnect message, with that login, to DuelingBook's duel server (a secure wss:// address on duelingbook.com; any other address gets nothing), closes that connection, and presses DUEL. Spellspeed never stores, logs or sends your login anywhere else.

Settings stored locally

Feature switches are saved in your browser's local extension storage. Viewer appearance and position are saved in DuelingBook's local storage. The selected viewer mode is saved in tab session storage. These preferences are not sent to the developer. Uninstalling removes extension storage; site preferences can be removed by clearing DuelingBook site data.

Actions and external links

Requested game actions use DuelingBook's existing connection and are visible according to that site's normal game behavior. The Fifty Fifty? helper sends choices and short coordination messages through ordinary DuelingBook duel chat; they may appear in its duel log or replays. Spellspeed does not provide a separate messaging server.

Clicking a replay link opens DuelingBook. Clicking YGOResources opens its site with the selected card name in the search link. No YGOResources lookup is made until you click. Those services handle requests under their own policies.

No analytics or advertising

Spellspeed has no analytics, advertising, tracking service, developer-operated backend or data sales. It does not upload browsing history, card histories, account details or duel content to the developer or an analytics provider.

Permissions

The storage permission saves your settings. Content scripts run only on https://duelingbook.com/ and https://www.duelingbook.com/ to provide the extension's DuelingBook features. All executable extension code is included in the package.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, write to contact@spellspeed.com or use the publisher contact shown on Spellspeed's Chrome Web Store listing. Changes to this policy will be reflected in its effective date and included with extension updates.
