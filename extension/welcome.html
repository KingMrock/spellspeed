Where things are

  • Settings: the Spellspeed button in your browser's toolbar. Pin it so it stays in view.
  • In a duel: drag cards onto zones, right-click or hover a card for its actions, double-click a pile to open it.
  • In the deck builder: point at a card to read it and see what gets it out of the Deck. Opening odds and Siding patterns are beside Solo.
  • In a replay: the timeline at the bottom goes to any play.
